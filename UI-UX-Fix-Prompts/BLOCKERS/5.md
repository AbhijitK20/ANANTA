## 2026-09-27T01:35:00+05:30 objectiveFast and objectiveNaive disagree by about 2.9, and the components table says why
- blocked on: `lib/engine/scoring/components.ts` and `lib/engine/validation/components-naive.ts`
- symptom: on a real five stop plan the two independent derivations of the same scalar differ by 2.8956, against a tolerance of 1e-6. The per-component table from `compareDrift` is the diagnosis: `novelty` fast 0.675 against naive -0.225 (a sign flip), `weather` fast 0.5 against naive 0, `value` fast 0.8 against naive 0.4 (exactly double), `crowd` fast -0.6 against naive -0.3 (exactly double), `groupFit` fast 0 against naive 0.4, `travelFriction` fast -0.02625 against naive 0.1368. The gap grows with stop count: 0.65 at one stop, 1.94 at three, 5.31 at eight. The weights on `ctx.profile.weights` are identical on both sides, so this is the arithmetic and not a weight mismatch.
- why i cannot fix it: sessions 4 and 6 own those two files, and `lib/**` is frozen for this round. I am reporting it because the stamp renders it and a reader will see a red failure on the demo path.
- proposed one-line fix: the four `Components` ids in the table above are the four to reconcile first. `value` and `crowd` being exactly double suggests one side is applying the weight twice. `novelty` flipping sign suggests one side is treating it as a per-stop signed component when the contract makes it an aggregate penalty only. `weather` returning 0.5 rather than 0 when `ctx.weatherSeverity` is null contradicts section 3 of the engine contract, which says weather fit is 0 when the weather is unknown.
- severity: blocker

What this means for the screen: the validation stamp is built to fail loudly, so `/trips` currently shows "Do not rely on this plan" with the component table open. That is the designed behaviour for a drifted plan and I would rather ship it that way than soften the threshold. The stamp is also the fastest triage tool the project has: one render now names the four components to look at, which is the whole reason the contract asked for the per-component table.
