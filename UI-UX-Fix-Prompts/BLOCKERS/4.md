- blocked on: `components/ananta/provenance-badge.tsx:65-69`

- proposed one-line fix: in `components/ananta/provenance-badge.tsx` change `field: Parameters<typeof FIELD_LABEL>[keyof typeof FIELD_LABEL];` to `field: keyof typeof FIELD_LABEL;` and import `ProvenancedField` from `@/lib/engine`. This unblocks my result card and the detail page both.
