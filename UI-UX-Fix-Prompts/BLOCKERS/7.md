## 2026-09-27 `.lift:hover` overwrites a resting depth, so depth and hover cannot coexist on one element

- proposed one-line fix: make the hover rule additive rather than replacing, by moving the resting depth into a custom property and composing it, or simply give controls their own class. The smallest change that removes the footgun is to scope the existing rule to cards: `.lift-card:hover, .lift-card:focus-within { ... }`, and leave `.lift` for elements with no depth state of their own. My four controls are already correct without it, so this is for the sessions that come after me.
