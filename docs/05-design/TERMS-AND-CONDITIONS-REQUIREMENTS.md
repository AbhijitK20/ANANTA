# Terms and Conditions Page Requirements

This is a product and content requirement, not final legal advice. The page must be reviewed before public launch.

## Page Requirements

The public site must clearly explain:

- Platform purpose and supported geography
- Traveler account responsibilities
- Provider listing and data responsibilities
- Event, price, opening-hours, and availability limitations
- Recommendations as decision support, not guarantees
- External videos, links, creator content, and third-party services
- Difference between booking simulation and a real booking
- Cancellation, refund, and provider-contact boundaries
- User-generated content and reporting
- Prohibited use
- Intellectual property
- Disclaimers and limitation of liability
- Account suspension and termination
- Governing law and jurisdiction
- Contact details and effective date

## Required Product Truth

- Do not call a simulated booking a confirmed booking.
- Do not promise live availability unless the system actually confirms it.
- Do not transfer responsibility for provider-submitted facts without explaining the platform's review role accurately.
- Do not claim that external videos represent current conditions.
