# Privacy Policy Page Requirements

This is a product and content requirement, not final legal advice. Replace all placeholders with confirmed owner, contact, jurisdiction, and deployment details before publishing.

## Page Requirements

The public site must provide a clearly linked Privacy Policy page covering:

- What account and profile information is collected
- Location permission, manual destination entry, and precise-location retention
- Traveler preferences, saved plans, reviews, reports, and provider submissions
- External media links and embedded YouTube or Instagram content
- Cookies, analytics, and similar technologies if enabled
- Third-party services and their roles
- Data retention and deletion requests
- Security practices stated accurately
- User rights applicable to the actual operating jurisdiction
- Contact details for privacy requests
- Policy update process and effective date

## Product Truth Requirements

- Do not claim data is collected if the implementation does not collect it.
- Do not claim encryption, certification, or legal compliance without verification.
- Explain that external media platforms may process interactions independently.
- Explain whether location is optional and what features work without it.
- Explain whether bookings are simulated or real.
