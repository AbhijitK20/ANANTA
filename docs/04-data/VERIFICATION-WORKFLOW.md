# Verification Workflow

## Review Queue Types

- New provider
- New experience
- New event
- Social/community discovery
- Duplicate candidate
- Stale record
- Incorrect-information report
- Hidden-gem candidate
- Changed or cancelled event
- External media submission
- Broken or misleading media report

## Review Steps

1. Inspect source and raw reference.
2. Compare duplicate candidates.
3. Verify location and structured details.
4. Confirm price, hours, availability, and suitability where possible.
5. Assign confidence and next-check time.
6. Publish, request changes, mark stale, or archive.
7. Record reviewer and audit details.

## Human Review Rules

- Social discovery cannot become confirmed event availability without confirmation.
- A video cannot be attached to a place or event without verifying the location match.
- Deleted, private, unavailable, or misleading media must be archived.
- Media metadata must preserve creator attribution and source URL.
- Safety-sensitive or environmentally sensitive locations require extra review.
- AI may summarize or suggest fields but cannot approve important records alone.
- User reports lower confidence until reviewed; they do not automatically punish providers.
