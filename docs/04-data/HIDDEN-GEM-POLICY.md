# Hidden Gem Policy

## Definition

A hidden gem is a promising local experience with positive evidence and relatively low exposure. It is not simply a place with few reviews.

## Candidate Signals

- Local recommendation or provider submission
- High satisfaction relative to exposure
- Authenticity and uniqueness
- Low tourist concentration
- Reliable provider
- Fresh price, hours, and availability
- Suitable access and safety information

## Labels

Use cautious labels:

- Hidden Gem Candidate
- Local Favorite
- Less Crowded
- Community Recommended
- Newly Discovered
- Authentic Local Experience

## Safety and Community Impact

Do not reveal exact coordinates when promotion could cause overcrowding, trespass, ecological damage, or harm to residents. Respect private property, cultural sensitivity, and community preferences.

## Approval

Every hidden-gem candidate needs source provenance, reviewer, verification date, confidence, and a recheck schedule.
